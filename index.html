Free tips for govt exam preparation

  1. Read the syllabus first. Study only what the exam asks.
  2. Make a daily timetable and keep to it, even if it is only 4 hours.
  3. Solve previous years' papers early, not only at the end.
  4. Revise every week. Notes you never read again are wasted effort.
  5. Read the newspaper daily for current affairs, and write short notes.
  6. Take a full mock test every week and review every mistake.
